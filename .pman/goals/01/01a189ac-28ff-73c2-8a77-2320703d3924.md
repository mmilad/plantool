## Goal
The Planus Manus UI is reviewed in recurring planning rounds and evolved step by step into a clear, efficient workspace. fp.dev is the provisional visual direction and Jira the workflow reference; pman stays independent.

## Way of working
- Regularly review the app and the existing plan against the visible UI and the pman data.
- Document problems and improvement ideas only under docs/*.
- Represent concrete work in the planning app: tickets under the UI programme, goals/plans linked to the matching ticket IDs.
- Respect existing ownership when adding or refining work.
- Planning rounds do not change product or source files.

## Target state
A traceable, current UI plan with consistent ownership; documented observations; concrete, prioritisable tickets including a local icon system, navigation, ticket views, overview and responsive, accessible use.
