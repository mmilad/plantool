## Goal
The pman web UI follows Jira's clear information architecture and efficient workflow while keeping its own calm visual identity. Tickets and planning documents are quick to find and pleasant to edit.

## Guardrails
- Jira is the reference for navigation, ticket overviews, filters and detail views; no pixel copy and no Jira trademarks or proprietary assets.
- One consistent icon system for navigation, ticket kinds, statuses and actions. Prefer local inline SVG icons, without external assets or new runtime dependencies.
- Existing project boundaries and security requirements of the local UI stay in place.
- Agree on the direction using a concrete UI concept before implementation.

## Acceptance criteria
- Navigation and work areas are clearly structured and work on desktop and narrow screens.
- Ticket list/tree, search/filter and ticket detail form an understandable workflow.
- Icons are consistent, local, accessibly labelled, and support rather than replace text.
- Goals, plans and decisions are reachable and visually distinct.
- The UI feels coherent and shows a helpful project overview even in its initial state.
